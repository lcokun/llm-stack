# 00NN — <title>

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by 00NN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are in play, what constraints apply, and what is wrong with the status quo.
Enough that someone reading this cold understands why a decision was needed.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated imperatively.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it commits us to. Include the costs
honestly. An ADR that only lists benefits is a sales pitch rather than a record.

## Alternatives considered

Each one, and the specific reason it lost. If an alternative was rejected for a reason
that could change later, say what would have to change.

---

**Rules for this directory**

1. One decision per file. Numbered sequentially, never renumbered.
2. An accepted ADR is never edited. If a decision changes, write a new ADR and set this
   one's status to `superseded by 00NN`. The record should show how the thinking evolved
   rather than pretending it was always correct.
3. Record the alternatives *and* the reasoning that killed them. The reasoning is the
   part that stops the same argument recurring six weeks later.
4. If a previous proposal was wrong, say so in the ADR. "My first proposal was X and
   here is why it failed scrutiny" is the most useful sentence in the file.
